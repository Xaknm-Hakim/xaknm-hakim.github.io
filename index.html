PROJECT-01 PRODUCT / V3.2 IMPLEMENTED

PersonalHub

A privately hosted, single-owner productivity platform that brings tasks, assignments, projects, notes, and planning into one canonical workspace.

The application combines a Next.js and TypeScript interface with domain services, Prisma/PostgreSQL, a versioned scoped-token API, and deployment, backup, and recovery tooling. Its manual Google Calendar projection is one-way and limited to eligible dated tasks and assignments.

  • TypeScript
  • PostgreSQL

  • Ansible
